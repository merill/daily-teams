With a Teams admin policy, you can add your custom privacy statement to Teams meeting recordings and transcripts in your organization. If your organization has a legal website that you want people to go to for any legal concerns, this policy allows you to display the URL. To learn more, see Manage Teams recording policies for meetings and events.

Audio announcements for recording and transcription

You can enable audio announcements to notify users when recording or transcription starts in Teams meetings and VoIP calls. Announcements help participants stay informed and improve accessibility awareness. Whether a user hears an announcement depends on that user's assigned policy, not just the meeting organizer's policy.

Use the -RecordingAndTranscriptionAudioNotification parameter in Set-CsTeamsMeetingPolicy for meetings and group calls, and in Set-CsTeamsCallingPolicy for 1:1 VoIP calls. The default value is Disabled. Set it to Enabled to turn on announcements, or Disabled to turn them off.

For example, to enable announcements in the global meeting and calling policies, run:

Set-CsTeamsMeetingPolicy -Identity Global -RecordingAndTranscriptionAudioNotification Enabled
Set-CsTeamsCallingPolicy -Identity Global -RecordingAndTranscriptionAudioNotification Enabled

Changes to the global policies apply to users who don't have custom policies assigned. To enable announcements for specific users or groups instead, configure custom meeting and calling policies and assign those policies to the users or groups.

Supported clients include Windows and macOS desktop, web, Microsoft Teams Rooms, Android, iOS, and VDI 2.0. VDI 1.0 isn't supported.

Note

This policy doesn't control audio announcements for PSTN users or for Teams users in 1:1 calls between Teams and PSTN. Audio announcements for these scenarios are enabled by default.

Require participant agreement for recording

With a Teams meeting policy, you can require participants to agree to being recorded before they can unmute or turn on their camera. Consent results are stored in the meeting attendance report. For details, see Require participant agreement for recording.
