Saturday, 7 November 2026

Day in brief

Capability-based event labels now replace webinar and town hall names across Teams guides

The period is dominated by a coordinated terminology pass across event administration. Microsoft Learn now uses “Events up to 1k attendees” and “Events optimized for a large audience” in policy references and event guides, while individual pages rename anonymous-attendance, transcription, and event-chat controls. The recording guidance also distinguishes events with up to 1,000 attendees from large-audience events and clarifies policy assignment.

  • Teams · Policies and settings
    Policy references adopt audience-size labels

    The Settings Policies Reference replaces webinar and town hall terminology with the exact labels “Events up to 1k attendees” and “Events optimized for a large audience” across related settings.

  • Meetings and events · General
    Recording guidance separates event audience sizes

    The Meeting Recording article now groups webinars and town halls under events, documents recording settings for events with up to 1,000 attendees or for large audiences, and clarifies policy-assignment instructions.

  • Meetings and events · General
    Anonymous-attendance steps use the new event labels

    The Anonymous Users In Meetings guide now refers to “Who can attend events up to 1k attendees” and “Who can attend events optimized for a large audience,” with corresponding updated configuration steps.

  • Meetings and events · General
    Caption controls retain defaults under renamed toggles

    Meeting Transcription Captions replaces the webinar and town hall toggle names with attendee-based labels and clarifies policy-assignment steps. Both settings remain On by default.

  • Meetings and events · General
    Event chat receives an audience-based setting name

    The dropdown label “Town hall chat experience” is now “Events optimized for a large audience chat experience.” Its options remain Optimized (default) and None.
