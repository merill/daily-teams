Wednesday, 25 November 2026

Day in brief

Teams expands compose-box Markdown support with a mid-December preview

Microsoft lists expanded Markdown support for the Teams compose box in public preview from mid-December 2026, with general availability beginning in mid-January 2027. Other meaningful updates refine external-bot controls, synthetic-media provider certification, and Teams Phone guidance around voice applications and click-to-call capacity; many remaining Teams Phone edits standardize terminology and improve setup documentation.

  • Teams · General
    Markdown adds visible syntax, toolbar, preview, and rich-text switching

    Microsoft Teams will let users write with visible Markdown syntax, use a Markdown toolbar, preview messages, and switch between Markdown and rich text. Public preview starts in mid-December 2026, followed by general availability in mid-January 2027.

  • External access · General
    External-bot guidance documents registration status and lobby policy paths

    The updated guidance distinguishes registered and unverified bots, explains their lobby placement, and describes policies that require approval or automatically block detected bots. Configuration is documented under Meetings > Meeting policies > Meeting join & lobby; with approval enabled, lobby settings can limit admission to organizers and co-organizers.

  • Meetings and events · General
    Synthetic-media guidance lists Pindrop as certified and two partners in progress

    The meetings guidance now lists Pindrop as certified, while GetReal Security and Reality Defender are still completing certification. It also names their Teams apps and directs admins to Teams apps > Manage apps when locating them.

  • Teams Phone · General
    Teams Phone getting-started guidance shifts to “voice applications”

    The reference now replaces specific references to Teams Phone Agents, auto attendants, and call queues with “voice applications” across planning, setup, service numbers, creation, and authorized-user guidance. Administrators should use that broader terminology when following the setup material.

  • Teams Phone · General
    Click-to-call guidance now states a 40-per-minute tenant-wide maximum

    The limits page now documents a maximum of 40 click-to-call calls per minute across all voice applications. Administrators should account for the broader tenant-wide scope when planning click-to-call traffic.
