Week in brief

Teams adds AI call delegation and synthetic-media defenses while ending iOS 17 support

The period’s main administrator changes span Teams Phone, meeting security, and mobile support. Intelligent call delegation is entering public preview; certified third-party synthetic audio and video detection is due in meetings in late November; and new Teams releases will require iOS/iPadOS 18 or later from mid-December. Security teams also gain a new Advanced Hunting table for Teams message snippets and metadata, while recording announcements become configurable through meeting and calling policies.

  • Teams · General
    iOS 17 and iPadOS 17 support ends in December

    Starting in mid-December 2026, new Teams releases will require iOS/iPadOS 18 or later. Existing Teams versions will continue working but without support. Microsoft recommends notifying users, encouraging OS upgrades, updating policies, and revising support documentation.

  • Teams Phone · AI and Copilot
    Intelligent call delegation enters Teams Phone public preview

    A new guide describes intelligent call delegation, an AI-powered voice agent for handling incoming calls. Enabling it requires eligible users to have a Microsoft Copilot license, Copilot Credits usage-based billing and a spending policy, plus the relevant personal attendant policy.

  • Teams · General
    Teams meetings add certified third-party synthetic-media detection

    Starting in late November 2026, organizations can integrate certified third-party solutions that detect synthetic audio and video in meetings. Administrators must deploy provider apps, grant permissions, and configure meeting policies; detection is performed by the third-party providers.

  • Teams · General
    Defender Advanced Hunting adds a MessageContents table for Teams

    Microsoft Defender for Office 365 is introducing a MessageContents table containing Teams message snippets and metadata, with rollout running from late September through late November 2026. Access is controlled by specific permissions for authorized security analysts.

  • Teams · General
    Recording and transcription announcements become policy-controlled

    Teams supports an optional audio announcement when recording or transcription starts. Administrators control it through meeting and calling policies; the setting is off by default, rolls out worldwide from May 2026 through January 2027, and does not support compliance recording.

For Teams administrators

Plan user communications and support updates for the iOS/iPadOS 18 requirement. For intelligent call delegation, eligible users need Microsoft Copilot licenses, Copilot Credits usage-based billing with a spending policy, and the relevant personal attendant policy. Synthetic-media detection requires provider apps, permissions, and meeting-policy configuration. Review access for analysts using the new MessageContents table. Recording announcements are optional and off by default; compliance recording is unsupported.
