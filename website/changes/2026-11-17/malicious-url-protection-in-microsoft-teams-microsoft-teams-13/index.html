Malicious URL protection in Microsoft Teams - Microsoft Teams

17 November 2026Feature update+58 / −58 lines

In brief

The documentation renames Link Protection to malicious URL protection and adds coverage for URLs embedded in QR codes. It also documents tenant-level administration and the behavior for new, existing, GCC, GCC High, and DoD tenants.

What Teams admins need to know

Protection is enabled by default. Administrators can manage it under Teams admin center > Messaging > Messaging settings > Messaging safety; no mandatory change is stated.

User experienceManage malicious URL protection in Teams

For new tenants, this protection is included as part of Teams baseline security protection and doesn’t require admin configuration.

For existing tenants and GCC, GCC High, and DoD tenants, admins can turn malicious URL protection off or on in the Teams admin center. It's enabled by default.

To turn off or turn on malicious URL protection:

  1. Sign in to the Teams admin center.

  2. In the left navigation, go to Messaging > Messaging settings.

  3. Under Messaging safety, turn off or turn on Malicious URL protection.

  4. Select Save.

This is a tenant-level setting. When enabled, all users in your organization have malicious URL protection applied to their Teams messages.
