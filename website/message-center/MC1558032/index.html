Microsoft Teams: Intelligent call recap for call queues in the Queues app on mobile

17 November 2026MC1558032 on mc.merill.net ↗Message

In brief

Microsoft Teams mobile's Queues app will add intelligent call recap for recorded and transcribed call queue calls starting early December 2026. Users can view AI-generated notes, key points, and follow-up tasks without replaying calls. No admin action is needed, but recording, transcription, and licenses should be confirmed.

Message Center announcement

What and why

We're introducing intelligent call recap for call queue calls in the Queues app on Microsoft Teams mobile. This update extends the experience already available on desktop, allowing calling representatives and supervisors to review AI-generated notes, key discussion points, and recommended follow-up tasks without replaying recordings or reading full transcripts.

Rollout schedule

  • General Availability (Worldwide): Beginning in early December 2026 and expected to complete by mid-December 2026

Impact on your organization

Who is affected

  • Calling representatives and supervisors who use the Queues app on Microsoft Teams mobile and have access to recorded and transcribed call queue calls

Platforms and services

  • Microsoft Teams mobile
  • Queues app

What will happen

After rollout:

  • Users can open a recorded call from shared call history and select View recap to review AI-generated notes, key discussion points, and recommended follow-up tasks.

  • Recap is available only for recorded and transcribed call queue calls and to users who already have access to the recording.
  • The feature does not change how call recordings are created, stored, or accessed.

Action required and recommendations

No admin action is required before rollout.

We recommend that you:

  • Confirm that recording and transcription are enabled for call queues where you want users to access intelligent call recap.
  • Review license assignments for users who will use the feature.

Learn more

  • Intelligent recap for Teams calls, meetings, and events - Microsoft Teams | Microsoft Learn

Compliance considerations

Organizations should review their existing recording, transcription, and AI-related compliance requirements as appropriate.
