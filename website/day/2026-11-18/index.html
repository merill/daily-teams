Teams support for iOS 17 and iPadOS 17 ends in mid-December 2026

The clearest administrator deadline is Teams ending support for iOS 17 and iPadOS 17 in mid-December 2026; new releases will then require version 18 or later, while existing versions continue working without support. New Teams Phone guidance details Frontier-only personal and shared voicemail AI triage, including transcript summaries, categories, suggested actions, urgency indicators, billing, and policy controls. A separate guide places intelligent call delegation in public preview. Updated meeting guidance covers consecutive interpretation with Copilot Credits, while a Message Center notice schedules external presenters for event group chat in late March 2027.

  • Teams · General
    iOS 17 and iPadOS 17 leave the supported Teams baseline

    Microsoft says Teams support for iOS 17 and iPadOS 17 ends starting in mid-December 2026. New Teams releases will require iOS or iPadOS 18 or later; existing app versions will continue working without support.

  • Teams Phone · AI and Copilot
    Frontier-only voicemail AI triage gets setup and billing guidance

    The new Teams Phone overview describes AI analysis of voicemail transcripts to produce summaries, categories, suggested actions, and urgency indicators. Personal triage can be configured in the Teams admin center or with PowerShell; shared triage uses the AI triage template, and the shared voicemail Queues app requires Teams Premium. Availability is limited to the Microsoft Frontier Program, with no GA timing announced.

  • Teams Phone · AI and Copilot
    Intelligent call delegation enters public preview

    A new guide describes intelligent call delegation as an AI-powered voice agent for handling incoming calls, managed through Teams personal attendant policies. Eligible users need a Microsoft Copilot license, Copilot Credits usage-based billing and a spending policy, plus the applicable personal attendant policy.

  • Meetings and events · AI and Copilot
    Interpreter adds consecutive interpretation with Copilot Credits controls

    Updated Teams Interpreter guidance says the capability supports both simultaneous and consecutive interpretation. Consecutive interpretation uses Copilot Credits, requires an eligible meeting organizer and spending policy, and provides interpreted audio to all participants.

  • Teams · General
    External presenters are coming to event group chat in March 2027

    Teams event group chat will support external presenters starting in late March 2027. The scenario requires Enable Green room and external collaboration, introduces cross-tenant chat, and does not store new customer data.

Microsoft Teams: External presenter support for event group chat

New

Microsoft Teams will support external presenters in event group chat starting late March 2027. This allows external presenters to join and coordinate in event chats if "Enable Green room" and external collaboration are enabled. The change introduces cross-tenant chat without storing new customer data.

New

Microsoft Teams is introducing real-time call queue metrics in the Queues app on Mobile, allowing calling representatives and supervisors to monitor queue activity and performance on the go. This rollout begins in December 2026, requires no admin action, and enhances visibility without changing queue management.
