Aa Cq Reference Prerequisites Licensing

25 November 2026Feature update+3 / −3 lines

In brief

The prerequisites page now refers to Direct Routing service numbers for voice applications, supported only for Teams users and representatives (agents), instead of specifically auto attendants and call queues. Wording and the page date were also updated.

What Teams admins need to know

Use the revised voice-application scope when validating Direct Routing service-number support.
