Administrators setting up Foundry voice agents should follow the revised procedure. Assign a phone number to the resource account when enabling calls from outside the Teams tenant.

Microsoft Foundry is the enterprise AI platform to build, ground, and govern AI apps and agents at scale. It brings together your full agent lifecycle with open development, built-in intelligence, and consistent security, compliance, and policy controls across every agent.

Direct dialVoice enabling Microsoft Foundry voice agents through Microsoft Teams Phone allow you to create conversational AI bots and attach them directly to a Teams Phone Agent enable direct calling by assigning a phone number via Teams Phone's resource accounts.number. By using these first-party conversational voice agents, you can significantly enhance your current Interactive Voice Response experiences or upgrade your current call flows.

By front-ending your call flow with a Microsoft Foundry voice agent, you unlock deep automation and tailored experiences. You can design custom workflows to handle common inquiries, such as billing, order status, and inventory checks. The agent acts as an intelligent first point of contact, resolving issues independently before seamlessly routing the call to a human representative using traditional Teams Phone routing systems if needed.
