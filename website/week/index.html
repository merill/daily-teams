Week in brief

Teams changes event recording controls as off-network Teams Free invitations retire

Message Center leads this week with two meeting-policy behavior changes: town-hall and webinar recording access will use RecordingForTownhall or RecordingForWebinar instead of AllowCloudRecording, while meeting recording and transcription choices will reflect assigned policies and add “Record only.” Off-network invitations to create Teams Free accounts retire in December 2026. Other notable rollouts include OneDrive video sharing in Teams events and malicious QR-code detection. Most Learn updates are operational clarifications; the main device-management exception is the PMP Admin Agent release and planned TAC transition.

  • Teams · Policies and settings
    Town hall and webinar recording access is decoupled from AllowCloudRecording

    For Events App town halls and webinars, “Who has access to the recording or transcript” will depend on RecordingForTownhall or RecordingForWebinar instead of AllowCloudRecording. Worldwide rollout is scheduled for early to mid-November 2026, with no action required.

  • Teams · Policies and settings
    Meeting recording options now reflect assigned policies

    Organizers will see automatic recording and transcription choices allowed by their assigned policies, including a new “Record only” option. Rollout starts in mid-November 2026 and completes by mid-December; no admin action is required.

  • Teams · General
    Teams Free off-network invitations retire in December 2026

    Users will no longer be able to invite external people by email or chat links to create Teams Free accounts. Existing Teams Free collaboration and federation remain unchanged; no admin action is required, but user guidance should be updated.

  • Devices · General
    PMP Admin Agent release adds Android fixes ahead of TAC transition

    The Certified Device Apps guidance identifies version 1.0.0.202610190119 (Admin Agent 901) as adding critical Android 15 connectivity and provisioning fixes for PMP, with rollout accelerated across Teams-certified devices. The update cannot be paused and is required for PMP management; TAC device-management deprecation is targeted for mid-November 2026.

  • Teams · General
    Teams adds malicious QR-code warnings in chats and channels

    Starting in mid-November 2026, Teams will detect malicious QR codes, warn users about harmful links, and blur external QR codes by default. The capability uses existing Malicious URL protection and requires no admin action.

For Teams administrators

No tenant configuration change is required for the highlighted Message Center rollouts. Administrators should update user guidance for the December 2026 Teams Free invitation retirement and account for organizers seeing only policy-allowed recording and transcription choices. For Teams Android device management, Admin Agent 1.0.0.202610190119 cannot be paused and is required for PMP management; administrators should prepare for TAC device-management deprecation targeted for mid-November 2026.
