Bluetooth Devices

20 November 2026Feature update+12 / −3 lines

In brief

The documentation now covers certified dual-mode Bluetooth peripherals, improved voice fidelity on compatible Windows 11 PCs, and Spatial Audio requirements including LE Audio and Super Wideband Stereo support.

What Teams admins need to know

Administrators can use these requirements to evaluate Bluetooth peripherals and Windows 11 PC compatibility for Teams audio experiences.

PCs using aftermarket USB Bluetooth adapters are not recommended or certified. USB Bluetooth audio dongles that connect to the PC as a USB Audio device instead of a Bluetooth controller cannot support the Native Bluetooth experience in Microsoft Teams.

To check if your PC supports Bluetooth LE Audio, but the latter mode is not certified for Teams.see Check if a Windows 11 device supports Bluetooth Low Energy Audio

To see a list of supported Bluetooth personal peripherals, see Certified personal peripherals.

Requirements for Spatial Audio on Native Bluetooth devices

Spatial Audio is only available for Bluetooth LE Audio connections, and only when the PC supports Super Wideband Stereo. To check if your PC supports Super Wideband Stereo, visit Configuring Bluetooth LE Audio quality settings on Windows 11

Troubleshooting

The performance of a peripheral in Native Bluetooth mode is impacted by the Bluetooth radio on the host PC. We recommend installing the latest firmware updates for both peripherals and Bluetooth radio.
