Month in brief

Teams will add Muted and Meeting chat sections in January 2027

November’s strongest Teams product changes are forward-looking: a January 2027 chat reorganization and an optional December Workflows template that links team tags to channel membership. Event administration guidance is also shifting from webinar and town hall terminology to capability-based labels. Outside those items, the period is largely documentation maintenance, with the most consequential exceptions affecting Teams Rooms deployment tooling and an Android Rooms layout issue.

  • Teams · General
    Muted and Meeting chats will reorganize conversations

    Teams plans to begin rollout in January 2027 of Muted chats, enabled by default, and Meeting chats, disabled by default. Users can enable, disable, and reorder the sections; muted conversations move automatically unless they are favorited. No administrator action is required.

  • Teams · General
    Workflows will add team-tag members to channels automatically

    A new optional Workflows app template, rolling out worldwide in mid-December 2026, will add users to channels when they are added to a team tag. The feature introduces no default changes and is suited to location- or project-based groups.

  • Meetings and events · Planning and deployment
    Event setup guidance adopts capability-based labels

    The setup guide now uses “Events up to 1k attendees” and “Events optimized for a large audience” throughout its policy tables and instructions instead of webinar and town hall terminology. The associated policy names and commands remain the same.

  • Teams Rooms · General
    Teams Rooms guidance no longer recommends the app update tool for new deployments

    The guidance states that the Teams Rooms app has included automatic updates since April 2024, making the app update tool unnecessary for new deployments. Administrators should review Intune configurations and consider unassigning the tool, except when deploying devices older than 2025.

  • Devices · Troubleshooting
    Large Gallery may be unavailable on a defined Android Rooms range

    The known-issues guidance identifies possible loss of the Large Gallery layout on Teams Rooms for Android app versions 1449/1.0.96.2026249711 through 1449/1.0.96.2026264504. The issue is expected to be addressed in a future update.

For Teams administrators

No tenant-wide change is required for the new chat sections or the optional tag workflow. Update event runbooks and policy references to use “Events up to 1k attendees” and “Events optimized for a large audience”; the associated policy names and commands remain unchanged. For Teams Rooms, review Intune assignments for the app update tool and consider unassigning it for new deployments, except where deploying devices older than 2025. Expect Large Gallery to be unavailable on the documented Android app range; no remediation is specified.

Microsoft Teams: New chat sections for muted and meeting chats

New

Microsoft Teams will introduce two new chat sections—Muted chats (enabled by default) and Meeting chats (disabled by default)—to organize conversations. Rollout begins January 2027. Users can enable/disable and reorder these sections; no admin action is needed. Muted chats move automatically to their section unless favorited.

Microsoft Teams: Decline incoming mobile calls with a message

New

Microsoft Teams mobile users will soon be able to decline incoming calls with a predefined or custom message sent via Teams chat. This feature, rolling out worldwide in December 2026, enhances communication without answering calls. It applies only to Teams-to-Teams calls with available chat and requires no admin action.
