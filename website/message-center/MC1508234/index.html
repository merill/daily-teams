Microsoft Teams: Automatic recording and transcription options align with admin policies

10 November 2026MC1508234 on mc.merill.net ↗Message

In brief

Microsoft Teams will update meeting options to show automatic recording and transcription settings based on assigned policies, adding a new "Record only" option. Rollout starts mid-November 2026, completing by mid-December. No admin action is needed; organizers see only options allowed by their policies.

Message Center announcement

Updated November 10, 2026: We have updated the timeline. Thank you for your patience. 

What and why

Microsoft Teams is updating the Record and transcribe automatically setting in Meeting options, so meeting organizers see only the automatic recording and transcription options permitted by their assigned Teams meeting policies. This change reduces confusion, improves policy compliance, and helps ensure organizers cannot select options that are unavailable based on organizational settings.

As part of this update, Teams will introduce a new Record only option that automatically starts meeting recording without starting transcription.

Rollout schedule

  • Targeted Release: Beginning in mid-November 2026 (previously early November) and expected to complete by end of November 2026 (previously mid-November)
  • General Availability (Worldwide): Beginning in early December 2026 and expected to complete by mid-December 2026

Impact on your organization

Who is affected

  • Teams administrators who manage meeting recording and transcription policies
  • Meeting organizers who configure automatic recording or transcription settings in Meeting options

Platforms and services

  • Teams desktop, web, and mobile clients that support Meeting Options.

What will happen

  • Meeting organizers will only see automatic recording and transcription options allowed by their effective Teams meeting policy.
  • Policy evaluation respects policies assigned directly to a user, inherited through group assignment, or applied through the global policy.
  • A new Record only option will be available when recording is permitted by policy.

  • The available automatic options for each policy combination are shown below:
Recording policyTranscription policyAutomatic options available in Meeting Options
AllowedAllowedRecord and transcribe; Transcribe only; Record only; Off
AllowedBlockedRecord only; Off
BlockedAllowedTranscribe only; Off
BlockedBlockedThe automatic setting is not shown

Action required and recommendations

No admin action is required; the feature is enabled automatically as part of the service update.

We recommend that:

  • Inform meeting organizers about the new Record only option.
  • Communicate that available automatic recording and transcription options are now determined by the organizer's assigned Teams meeting policies.
  • Review recording and transcription policies if you want to understand which options organizers in your organization will see.

Compliance considerations

No compliance considerations identified. Review as appropriate for your organization.
