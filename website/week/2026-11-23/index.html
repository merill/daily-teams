Week in brief

Teams mobile file access now requires newer app builds before April 2027

The week’s most consequential Teams changes are a mandatory mobile app update, a future shift in town hall and webinar recording permissions, expanded event-registration capacity, a new Omnissa Mac VDI optimization, and an eight-source NDI production switchboard. Most of the accompanying Learn updates are lower-impact Teams Phone documentation work: terminology is being broadened from auto attendants and call queues to voice applications, alongside refreshed screenshots, metadata, and examples. Meaningful reference exceptions include the stated lack of Team and channel analytics support in GCCH and DoD environments and a documented 40-click-to-call-per-minute tenant-wide limit across voice applications.

  • Teams · General
    Mobile file access gets a firm version deadline

    By April 1, 2027, Android users need Teams version 1416/1.0.0.2026151302 and iOS users need version 8.19.0 to retain file access. Older versions will lose file functionality as Teams replaces pre-authenticated URLs with Microsoft Entra ID authentication.

  • Teams · Policies and settings
    Town hall and webinar recording access changes setting behavior

    Starting in mid-January 2027, the “Who has access to the recording or transcript” option for Events App town halls and webinars will depend on RecordingForTownhall or RecordingForWebinar, rather than AllowCloudRecording. The notice says no action is required.

  • Teams · General
    Event registration capacity scales to 20,000

    Beginning in December 2026, town halls and webinars will support registration limits tied to attendee capacity: Teams Enterprise supports 3,000 registrations, while Attendee Capacity Packs can support up to 20,000. Rollout is scheduled from mid-December 2026 through late January 2027.

  • Teams · General
    Omnissa Mac VDI receives a SlimCore optimization

    A new Omnissa-on-Mac VDI optimization using the SlimCore media engine is scheduled to roll out from December 2026 through February 2027. Organizations need Horizon Client 2609.1 or later and Teams 26304.1207.5384.858 or later to benefit.

  • Teams · General
    NDI production adds an eight-source streaming switchboard

    Teams is introducing a switchboard for event producers to manage and stream up to eight NDI sources in meetings and events, with worldwide rollout from mid-November through mid-December 2026. Administrators must enable NDI streaming, and users must activate production capabilities in Teams.

For Teams administrators

Prioritize Teams mobile updates before April 1, 2027: Android must reach version 1416/1.0.0.2026151302 and iOS version 8.19.0 to preserve file access. For Omnissa Mac VDI, the documented benefit requires Horizon Client 2609.1 or later and Teams 26304.1207.5384.858 or later. NDI streaming requires admin enablement, followed by user activation of production capabilities. The registration-capacity and recording-access changes require no action according to the supplied Message Center notices, although administrators should understand that recording access will later be governed by the event-specific settings rather than AllowCloudRecording.
