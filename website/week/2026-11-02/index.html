Week in brief

Teams will add muted and meeting chat sections in January 2027

Teams’ most consequential update this week is a planned January 2027 chat reorganization: users will get separate Muted chats and Meeting chats sections. The period also brings an optional tag-driven channel workflow, a broad shift in event-policy terminology, and two Teams Rooms administration issues: the app update tool is no longer recommended for new deployments, and Large Gallery may be unavailable on a defined Android app range.

  • Teams · General
    New sections will organize muted and meeting chats

    Teams will introduce Muted chats, enabled by default, and Meeting chats, disabled by default, beginning in January 2027. Users can enable, disable, and reorder the sections; muted chats move automatically into their section unless they are favorited.

  • Teams · General
    A Workflows template will sync team tags with channels

    An optional Workflows app template will automatically add users to channels when they are added to a team tag. Worldwide rollout is scheduled for mid-December 2026, with no default changes, supporting location- or project-based groups.

  • Teams · Policies and settings
    Event policy references now use attendee-capacity labels

    The Settings Policies Reference now uses “Events up to 1k attendees” and “Events optimized for a large audience” instead of webinar and town hall terminology across related settings.

  • Teams Rooms · General
    Teams Rooms guidance no longer recommends the app update tool for new deployments

    The Autopilot Autologin guidance says Teams Rooms app automatic updates have been included since April 2024, so the app update tool is no longer recommended for new deployments. It advises reviewing Intune assignments and considering unassignment except when deploying devices older than 2025.

  • Devices · Troubleshooting
    Large Gallery may be unavailable on specific Android Rooms builds

    The known-issues page identifies a limitation on Teams Rooms for Android app versions 1449/1.0.96.2026249711 through 1449/1.0.96.2026264504. Administrators should expect Large Gallery to be unavailable on those builds while awaiting a future update.

For Teams administrators

Chat sections require no admin action. The tag workflow is optional and does not change defaults, so admins can assess it for location- or project-based groups rather than prepare a tenant-wide change. For Teams Rooms, review Intune assignments for the app update tool: the guidance says automatic app updates have been included since April 2024 and suggests considering unassignment except for devices older than 2025. Update event runbooks and policy references to the labels “Events up to 1k attendees” and “Events optimized for a large audience.” On affected Android Rooms versions, expect Large Gallery to be unavailable.

Microsoft Teams: New chat sections for muted and meeting chats

New

Microsoft Teams will introduce two new chat sections—Muted chats (enabled by default) and Meeting chats (disabled by default)—to organize conversations. Rollout begins January 2027. Microsoft Teams: Decline incoming mobile calls with a message

New

Microsoft Teams mobile users will soon be able to decline incoming calls with a predefined or custom message sent via Teams chat. This feature, rolling out worldwide in December 2026, enhances communication without answering calls. It applies only to Teams-to-Teams calls with available chat and requires no admin action.
