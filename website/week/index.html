Week in brief

Teams Free off-network invitations end in December 2026

Starting December 2026, users will no longer be able to invite external people by email or chat links to create Teams Free accounts; existing Teams Free collaboration and federation remain unchanged. During November, Teams is also rolling out direct OneDrive video sharing in events, policy-filtered recording and transcription choices, malicious QR-code detection, and expanded event-registration email links.

  • Teams · General
    Teams Free retires off-network invitations

    From December 2026, users cannot invite external people by email or chat links to create Teams Free accounts. Existing Teams Free collaboration and federation remain unchanged. Administrators should update user guidance; the notice specifies no admin action.

  • Teams · General
    Teams events gain direct OneDrive video sharing

    Beginning mid-November 2026, event organizers and producers can share videos directly from OneDrive through "Manage what attendees see", supporting smoother, higher-quality playback without screen sharing or PowerPoint embedding. No admin action is required.

  • Teams · Policies and settings
    Meeting recording choices now follow assigned policies

    Rolling from mid-November through mid-December 2026, meeting options will show automatic recording and transcription settings according to assigned admin policies and add a "Record only" option. Organizers see only policy-allowed choices; no admin action is needed.

  • Teams · General
    Teams adds malicious QR-code detection

    Starting mid-November 2026, Teams will detect malicious QR codes in chats and channels, warn users about harmful links, and blur external QR codes by default. The capability uses existing Malicious URL protection, so no admin action is required.

  • Teams · General
    Event organizers can email registration links

    The "Invite to register" capability is expanding worldwide by late November 2026 so organizers can email event registration links. No special preparation is needed, but Microsoft recommends configuring a custom domain for Microsoft 365 notifications to avoid restrictions.

For Teams administrators

Update user guidance before the Teams Free retirement; no tenant action is specified. The other announcements also require no special preparation, although Microsoft recommends configuring a custom domain for Microsoft 365 notification emails to avoid event-registration restrictions.
