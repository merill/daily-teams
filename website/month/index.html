Month in brief

Teams events gain OneDrive video sharing as policy controls and security rollouts expand

November’s strongest changes are operational: Teams events are gaining direct OneDrive video sharing, meeting recording and transcription options will reflect assigned policies, and off-network invitations to Teams Free will retire in December. Malicious QR-code detection is also rolling out in chats and channels through existing Malicious URL protection. For Teams-certified Android devices, an unpausable Admin Agent update is required for Pro Management Portal management as Microsoft prepares to deprecate TAC device management. Much of the remaining activity standardizes event terminology and refreshes deployment and troubleshooting guidance.

  • Teams · General
    Organizers can share OneDrive videos directly in Teams events

    Beginning in mid-November 2026, event organizers and producers can use “Manage what attendees see” to share videos from OneDrive, avoiding screen sharing or PowerPoint embedding and enabling smoother, higher-quality playback. No administrator action is required.

  • Teams · Policies and settings
    Meeting options align recording controls with assigned policies

    Rolling out from mid-November through mid-December 2026, automatic recording and transcription options will reflect each organizer’s assigned policies. The update adds a “Record only” option, and organizers will see only settings their policies allow.

  • Teams · General
    Off-network invitations to Teams Free retire in December

    Starting in December 2026, users will no longer be able to invite external people by email or chat link to create Teams Free accounts. Existing Teams Free collaboration and federation remain unchanged, but user guidance should be updated.

  • Teams · General
    Teams adds malicious QR-code detection to messages

    From mid-November 2026, Teams will detect potentially malicious QR codes in chats and channels, warn users about harmful links, and blur external QR codes by default. The capability is enabled through existing Malicious URL protection and requires no administrator action.

  • Devices · General
    A required Android agent update accelerates the move to PMP

    Certified Teams Android devices are receiving version 1.0.0.202610190119 (Admin Agent 901), which adds Android 15 connectivity and provisioning fixes. The update cannot be paused and is required for device management in the Pro Management Portal; the documentation also prepares administrators for TAC device-management deprecation targeted for mid-November 2026.

For Teams administrators

Update user guidance for the December Teams Free invitation retirement. No special administrator action is stated for OneDrive video sharing, policy-aligned recording options, or QR-code detection; organizers will see only recording settings allowed by their policies, and QR protection uses existing Malicious URL protection. For certified Android devices, account for the required Admin Agent update and prepare for the planned TAC device-management deprecation. Event runbooks should adopt the capability-based labels “Events up to 1k attendees” and “Events optimized for a large audience” where applicable.
