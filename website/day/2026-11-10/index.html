Day in brief

Teams Free off-network invitations retire as OneDrive video sharing reaches Teams events

The period brings a December 2026 retirement for off-network invitations to Teams Free, alongside mid-November rollouts for direct OneDrive video sharing in Teams events and policy-aligned recording controls. Teams also adds malicious QR-code detection through existing Malicious URL protection. The remaining updates are mainly Teams Rooms reference and hardware documentation changes.

  • Teams · General
    Off-network invitations to Teams Free will be retired

    Starting in December 2026, users will no longer be able to invite external people by email or chat links to create Teams Free accounts. Existing Teams Free collaboration and federation remain unchanged, but user-facing guidance should be updated.

  • Teams · General
    Teams events will support direct OneDrive video sharing

    Beginning in mid-November 2026, event organizers and producers can share OneDrive videos through “Manage what attendees see,” enabling playback without screen sharing or embedding the video in PowerPoint. No administrator action is required.

  • Teams · Policies and settings
    Meeting recording controls will reflect assigned policies

    From mid-November through mid-December 2026, meeting options will show automatic recording and transcription choices allowed by the organizer’s assigned policies, including a new “Record only” option.

  • Teams · General
    Teams will detect malicious QR codes in chats and channels

    Starting in mid-November 2026, Teams will warn about harmful QR-code links, with external QR codes blurred by default. The capability is enabled through existing Malicious URL protection and requires no separate administrator action.

  • Teams Rooms · General
    Teams Rooms references add PTZ support and a certified room kit

    The Teams Devices Feature Comparison now marks local pan-tilt-zoom controls as available in three rows, while the certified hardware list adds the AVer CORE500 Room Kit with a Core Ultra 5 processor, 16 GB of memory, and 256 GB of storage. These updates support device evaluation rather than signaling a new Teams Rooms rollout.
