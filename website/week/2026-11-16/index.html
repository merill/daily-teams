Week in brief

Teams Rooms management shifts to Pro portal as meeting AI controls split

Teams administrators are entering a deadline-heavy stretch: device management is moving from the Teams Admin Center to the Teams Rooms Pro Management Portal by November 2026, while December’s meeting rollout splits the Copilot meeting policy into MeetingAI and TranscriptionWithMeetingAI. The period also adds Frontier-only Teams Phone voicemail-triage billing guidance, flags possible BSODs on unsupported Windows Server 2019 VDI, and sets a November 30 retirement for PowerPoint Live slide translation.

  • Teams · General
    Teams device management is moving from TAC to the Teams Rooms Pro portal

    Teams Admin Center device management will be deprecated by November 2026 in favor of the Teams Rooms Pro Management Portal. Administrators must follow the migration steps, update device software, and meet device prerequisites; timing varies by device type and cloud environment.

  • Teams · AI and Copilot
    Meeting Copilot governance splits into MeetingAI and transcription policies

    Microsoft is replacing the single Copilot meeting policy with MeetingAI and TranscriptionWithMeetingAI, alongside more granular meeting options. Rollout begins in December 2026, and existing settings will be mapped automatically without an administrator migration action.

  • Teams Phone · AI and Copilot
    Frontier voicemail AI triage guidance adds billing and scope controls

    New Teams Phone guidance for personal voicemail AI triage documents Copilot licensing, usage-based billing, security-group scoping, spending policies, limits, and alerts. The capability is available only through the Microsoft Frontier Program, with no general-availability timing announced.

  • Clients · Planning and deployment
    Windows Server 2019 VDI now carries a documented Teams BSOD risk

    The updated VDI requirements guidance reports possible BSODs on Windows Server 2019 virtual machines running Teams 26288.x.x.x or later. Teams 26276.x.x.x is unaffected; the page also states that Windows Server 2019 is unsupported and customers must migrate to Windows Server 2025.

  • Teams · General
    PowerPoint Live retires Translate slides worldwide on November 30

    Starting November 30, 2026, attendees will no longer be able to translate slides during Teams meetings; slides will display in their original language. No administrator action is required, but organizations should notify users and update related training materials.

For Teams administrators

Prioritize the TAC-to-PMP migration by completing the required steps, updating device software, and verifying prerequisites before the November 2026 deprecation. Existing Copilot settings will map automatically to the new meeting policies, but administrators should review the resulting controls. For Frontier voicemail triage, validate licensing, usage-based billing, spending policies, and voicemail policies. Teams VDI estates on Windows Server 2019 should plan migration to Windows Server 2025; Teams 26276.x.x.x is identified as unaffected, while 26288.x.x.x or later may produce BSODs. Before November 30, notify users and update training for the retirement of Translate slides.
