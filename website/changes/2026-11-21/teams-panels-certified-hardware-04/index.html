Teams Panels Certified Hardware

21 November 2026Doc update+4 / −0 lines

In brief

The documentation now lists Cisco Navigator, TAP Scheduler, DockFlex, and TAP Scheduler 2, including their AOSP, Teams client, Intune, Authenticator, Admin Agent versions, and November 18–19, 2026 dates.

What Teams admins need to know

Administrators can use the updated version details when reviewing supported Teams panels hardware and software configurations.
