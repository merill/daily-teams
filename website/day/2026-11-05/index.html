Day in brief

Teams will organize muted and meeting chats into dedicated sections in January

Microsoft Teams will introduce a January 2027 chat reorganization: the Muted chats section will be enabled by default, while Meeting chats will be disabled by default, with users able to toggle and reorder both. Two worldwide changes are also planned for December 2026: a Workflows template that links team tags to channel membership and mobile call declines with chat messages. Documentation updates add certified-device entries and SlimCore troubleshooting detail.

  • Teams · General
    Muted and meeting chats get dedicated sections in January 2027

    Teams will add Muted chats and Meeting chats sections. Muted chats will be enabled by default, while Meeting chats will be disabled by default. Users can enable or disable and reorder the sections, and muted chats move automatically unless they are favorited. Rollout begins in January 2027, with no admin action required.

  • Teams · General
    Workflows will connect team tags to channel membership

    An optional Workflows app template will automatically add users to channels when they are added to a team tag. The feature rolls out worldwide in mid-December 2026 and does not change defaults.

  • Teams · General
    Teams mobile users will be able to decline calls with messages

    Users will be able to decline incoming Teams mobile calls with a predefined or custom message sent through Teams chat. Worldwide rollout is scheduled for December 2026; the feature applies only to Teams-to-Teams calls with available chat and requires no admin action.

  • Devices · General
    Certified Hardware Android adds Meeting Owl and Cisco entries

    The table now includes Meeting Owl 5 Pro, Owl Tablet, and multiple Cisco devices, along with their associated software versions and certification dates. Administrators can use these details when validating inventories and planning deployments.

  • Clients · Troubleshooting
    SlimCore troubleshooting adds Windows error 6/51 guidance

    Vdi 2 Troubleshooting now documents Windows error 6/51, ERROR_INVALID_HANDLE, during SlimCore download, deployment, or startup, and points administrators to BITS Client and AppXDeployment-Server logs.

New

Microsoft Teams will introduce two new chat sections—Muted chats (enabled by default) and Meeting chats (disabled by default)—to organize conversations. Rollout begins January 2027.
