Day in brief

Guest-invitation reporting and synthetic-media detection headline Teams security updates

The period’s most consequential changes are two late-November Teams security rollouts: users will be able to report suspicious guest invitations to IT admins, while organizations can integrate certified third-party detection for synthetic audio and video in meetings. Consecutive interpretation in Interpreter is also targeted for general availability by late 2026. Documentation updates add operational detail for malicious URL protection and duplicate-name dialing in Teams Phone.

  • Teams · Reports, alerts and monitoring
    Guest-invitation reporting reaches Teams in late November

    Starting late November 2026, users can report suspicious guest invitations for security concerns, with reports routed to IT admins through the Teams admin center. No action is needed when Report a security concern is already enabled in messaging policies.

  • Teams · General
    Teams meetings add certified third-party synthetic-media detection

    Starting late November 2026, organizations can integrate certified third-party detection for synthetic audio and video in meetings. Admins adopting a provider must deploy its app, grant permissions, and configure meeting policies; the detection itself is performed by the third party.

  • Teams · AI and Copilot
    Interpreter’s consecutive interpretation moves toward general availability

    The turn-by-turn AI spoken translation capability, currently in public preview, is expected to reach general availability by late 2026. It uses usage-based Copilot Credits billing, so organizations need spending policies and meeting organizers with eligible Copilot licenses.

  • Security and compliance · General
    Malicious URL protection guidance adds QR-code coverage and tenant scope

    The updated article uses malicious URL protection in place of Link Protection, documents URLs embedded in QR codes, and explains tenant-level administration for new, existing, GCC, GCC High, and DoD tenants. It directs administrators to Teams admin center > Messaging > Messaging settings > Messaging safety and states that protection is enabled by default.

  • Teams Phone · General
    Teams Phone guidance centers duplicate-name dialing on Enhanced search context

    The dial-by-name reference now directs administrators to Enhanced search context instead of the -UserNameExtension parameter when multiple users share a name. The available values are None, Office, and Department, which add identifying information to spoken prompts.

Microsoft Teams: Third-party synthetic audio and video detection in meetings

New

Microsoft Teams will support certified third-party synthetic audio and video detection in meetings starting late November 2026. Organizations can integrate these solutions to enhance security against AI-generated media threats. Admins must deploy provider apps, grant permissions, and configure meeting policies. Detection is performed by third parties, with Microsoft Teams enabling integration.

Microsoft Teams Town halls: Upload custom backgrounds in Manage what attendees see

New

Microsoft Teams will allow organizers and presenters of town hall events with Teams Premium licenses to upload custom background images in the "Manage what attendees see" feature. Rollout begins early June 2026, enabling branded visuals behind presenters and shared content in supported layouts, with no admin action needed.

Microsoft Teams: External presenters can manage event production controls

New

Microsoft Teams now allows event organizers to grant trusted external presenters (federated B2B and guest users) production control during events, enabling them to manage attendee viewing. This feature, rolling out August to November 2026, requires explicit organizer assignment and supports collaboration with external partners.

Microsoft Teams: Consecutive interpretation in Interpreter agent will be generally available

New

Consecutive interpretation in Microsoft Teams Interpreter, currently in public preview, will roll out to general availability by late 2026. It offers turn-by-turn AI-generated spoken translation using usage-based billing with Copilot Credits. Organizations must set spending policies and have eligible meeting organizers with Copilot licenses.

Microsoft Teams: Intelligent call recap for call queues in the Queues app on mobile

New

Microsoft Teams mobile's Queues app will add intelligent call recap for recorded and transcribed call queue calls starting early December 2026. Users can view AI-generated notes, key points, and follow-up tasks without replaying calls. No admin action is needed, but recording, transcription, and licenses should be confirmed.
