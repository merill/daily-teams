General

Latest General changes

10 November 2026

Autopilot Autologin

General

The documentation now identifies version 5.0.111.0 alongside the automatic update functionality included since April 2024.

10 November 2026

Certified Hardware

General

The certified hardware page now lists the AVer CORE500 Room Kit with a Core Ultra 5 processor, 16 GB memory, 256 GB storage, and a product link.

10 November 2026

Limits Specifications Teams

General

The documentation now states that limits may apply based on factors such as tenant age and calling activity. When reached, users may be temporarily unable to place outbound 1:1 calls to external users.

10 November 2026

Microsoft Teams: Invite attendees to register

Message CenterMC1537353 on mc.merill.net ↗Stay informed
General

Microsoft Teams is expanding the "Invite to register" feature for events, allowing organizers to email registration links. Rolling out worldwide by late November 2026. No special preparation needed, but configuring a custom domain for M365 notifications is recommended to avoid restrictions. No compliance issues identified.

10 November 2026

Microsoft Teams: Malicious QR code detection in messages

Message CenterMC1526651 on mc.merill.net ↗Stay informed
General

Microsoft Teams will detect malicious QR codes in messages starting mid-November 2026, warning users of harmful links. Detection applies to chats and channels, with external QR codes blurred by default. The feature is enabled via existing Malicious URL protection, requiring no admin action.

10 November 2026

Microsoft Teams: Retirement of off-network invitations to Teams Free

Message CenterMC1546409 on mc.merill.net ↗Major updatePlan for change
General

Microsoft Teams will retire off-network invitations for Teams Free accounts starting December 2026. Users can no longer invite external people via email or chat links to create Teams Free accounts. Existing Teams Free collaboration and federation remain unchanged. No admin action is needed, but user guidance should be updated.

10 November 2026

Microsoft Teams: Users can temporarily pause all notifications

Message CenterMC1465768 on mc.merill.net ↗Stay informed
General

Microsoft Teams will allow users to temporarily pause notifications for a chosen duration to reduce interruptions and improve focus. This feature, available on desktop, web, and mobile, rolls out globally from mid-September to November 2026. No admin action is needed; users can enable it via their profile menu.

10 November 2026

Microsoft Teams: Video sharing from OneDrive in Teams events

Message CenterMC1506734 on mc.merill.net ↗Major updatePlan for change
General

Microsoft Teams will soon allow event organizers and producers to share videos directly from OneDrive during Teams events using "Manage what attendees see," enabling smoother, higher-quality playback without screen sharing or embedding in PowerPoint. Rollout begins mid-November 2026, with no action required.

10 November 2026

Teams Devices Feature Comparison

General

The feature comparison table changes local pan tilt zoom (PTZ) controls from Not available to Available in three rows.

7 November 2026

Anonymous Users In Meetings

General

The article replaces references to “Who can attend webinars” and “Who can attend town halls” with “Who can attend events up to 1k attendees” and “Who can attend events optimized for a large audience,” including updated configuration steps.

7 November 2026

Manage Event Chat

General

The dropdown label changed from “Town hall chat experience” to “Events optimized for a large audience chat experience.” The options remain Optimized (default) and None.

7 November 2026

Manage Registration Form Events

General

The documentation now refers to the setting as **Events registration form questions** instead of **Webinar registration form questions**. The page metadata was also updated.

7 November 2026

Manage Vod Publishing

General

The setup steps now use revised names for webinar and town hall recording-publishing dropdowns, including audience-size descriptions.

7 November 2026

Meeting Recording

General

The article now groups webinars and town halls under events and documents the settings for recording events with up to 1,000 attendees or for large audiences. Policy assignment instructions were also clarified.

7 November 2026

Meeting Transcription Captions

General

The documentation replaces the webinar and town hall toggle names with attendee-based labels and clarifies policy-assignment steps. Both settings remain On by default.

6 November 2026

Aa Cq Reference Limits Supported Configurations

General

The reference table now lists several language locales in a different order, including Indonesian/Icelandic, Lithuanian/Latvian, and the Serbian-to-Spanish entries. The Spanish (Spain) audio link remains associated with es-ES.

6 November 2026

Autopilot Autologin

General

The Teams Rooms app has included automatic updates since April 2024, so the app update tool is no longer recommended for new deployments.

6 November 2026

Rooms Release Note

General

The release notes now document a fix for certain devices sending low-bitrate video in specific meeting scenarios.

5 November 2026

Certified Hardware Android

General

The table now includes Meeting Owl 5 Pro, Owl Tablet, and multiple Cisco devices with associated software versions and certification dates.

5 November 2026

Microsoft Teams: Automatically add tag members to channels with Workflows

Message CenterMC1540351 on mc.merill.net ↗Stay informed
General

Microsoft Teams will introduce a Workflows app template to automatically add users to channels when they are added to a team tag. This optional feature, rolling out worldwide in mid-December 2026, helps streamline access for location- or project-based groups without default changes.

5 November 2026

Microsoft Teams: Decline incoming mobile calls with a message

Message CenterMC1540349 on mc.merill.net ↗Stay informed
General

Microsoft Teams mobile users will soon be able to decline incoming calls with a predefined or custom message sent via Teams chat. This feature, rolling out worldwide in December 2026, enhances communication without answering calls. It applies only to Teams-to-Teams calls with available chat and requires no admin action.

5 November 2026

Microsoft Teams: New chat sections for muted and meeting chats

Message CenterMC1269864 on mc.merill.net ↗Major updatePlan for change
General

Microsoft Teams will introduce two new chat sections—Muted chats (enabled by default) and Meeting chats (disabled by default)—to organize conversations. Rollout begins January 2027. Users can enable/disable and reorder these sections; no admin action is needed. Muted chats move automatically to their section unless favorited.

5 November 2026

Shared Spaces Insights

General

The article now explains how to select a chart bar to view individual rooms, people counts, occupancy details, and comparisons with configured capacity.
